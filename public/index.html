About Biribi

Descriptions for coverage and ecosystem listings.

Short description

Biribi is an onchain roulette protocol on Arbitrum One with separate BRB, native USDC and DAI bankroll vaults. 18+. Bets and deposited capital can be lost.

Extended description

Biribi connects roulette players and bankroll liquidity providers on Arbitrum One. Players share a draw powered by Chainlink VRF. Randomness requests, results and payouts can be checked onchain. BRB, native USDC and DAI each have a separate bankroll vault. Liquidity providers share in that vault’s gains and losses. Withdrawals enter a queue, depend on available liquidity and processing, and incur fees. 18+. Availability depends on local law.
